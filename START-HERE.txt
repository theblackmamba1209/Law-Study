SINAG LEARNING STUDIO · EDITION 12

Start on a computer
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. For offline study, open index.html in a current desktop browser and unlock with your portable-copy password.
2. For live AI, use Python 3.9 or newer. Open Start-Windows.cmd or Start-Mac.command. Alternatively open a terminal in the extracted folder and run “py -3 Sinag.py” on Windows or “python3 Sinag.py” on macOS/Linux.
3. Choose OpenAI or Claude. Use a model enabled on your API account. Enter your provider API key in the launcher's hidden prompt. Keep the launcher window open. It serves only this computer at http://127.0.0.1:8765. You can also supply OPENAI_API_KEY or ANTHROPIC_API_KEY through your environment.
4. Unlock the app in the browser window that opens. In Learner mode, choose Study tutor, select your material and ask a question. For AI-assisted editing, open ••• (Workspace options), choose Enter editor mode, then open Study desk. Review the draft before saving. The displayed source and your request go to the selected provider. Its API usage charges apply.

Study a lesson
Save lesson details in Editor mode and open its Test tab. Empty Analysis, Critical thinking and Vocabulary tracks fill from the available lesson material. The offline generator uses templates, conditions, cases and glossary entries. An empty lesson needs study material first. Existing authored tracks take priority. Use Edit Analysis, Edit Critical thinking or Edit Vocabulary to add, delete, revise or reorder items. Saving pins the track to your version. Restore returns it to the automatic or original bank.
For an AI-written set, open Study desk inside the lesson. Choose the combined material, a case or an individual section and click Draft lesson tests. Check questions, options, answers and supporting quotes. Apply test bank replaces those three tracks together. Undo reverses the change. Essay scoring starts with a suggested rubric score that you can adjust.

Edit the workspace
The app starts in Learner mode. Open ••• (Workspace options) and choose Enter editor mode. Choose Return to learner mode from the same menu when finished. A distributed learner edition has no mode switch. Use Edit whole lesson or Edit this part to change the underlying material and Add to insert content blocks. Tables in the workbench and comparison editor support editing. Page wording under ••• changes displayed text and labels on the current study page. Those wording changes do not rewrite the underlying law, case or scoring logic.
Use Study desk to work on a field or block. Structured portions show their data as JSON. Use the usual lesson editor for a form view. For a comparison table, ask for rows separated by new lines and cells separated by |, then choose Add as table. Undo and Redo in ••• apply to edits made in the current session. Security settings and device credentials are managed through Security.

Move existing work
Before switching browser addresses or devices, choose Back up my work in your current installation. Open the new copy, unlock, enter Editor mode, and open My workbench → Restore from a backup. The package contains the material in your uploaded export. Browser-only progress and edits need that backup. Keep your original export until you confirm your progress and edits in the new copy.
For iPhone or iPad, use Sinag's existing HTTPS hosting or installation workflow for the upgraded index.html. The local desktop AI launcher does not serve the phone. Mobile live AI needs an authenticated same-origin /api/study backend. Automatic template-based practice works without it.
